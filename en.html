Amigos Seafood & Steak House

Amigos was born with a simple idea: to make Santiago feel like the capital of Dominican gastronomy. Every plate brings together three things we love: the fire of the grill, the freshness of the sea and the seasoning of our grandmothers.

This is where Santiago celebrates birthdays, graduations and anniversaries, closes deals and brings the whole family together. Like a friend's house, our doors are open every day.

Guinness World Records · July 2025
556kg

The world's largest mofongo.

In Santiago's Central Park, hundreds of people watched our team prepare a 556 kg (1,226.8 lb) mofongo. Guinness World Records certified it on the spot, and Amigos became the first Dominican restaurant to hold a Guinness World Record.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before your visit.

Where is Amigos Seafood & Steak House?
We are on Salvador Estrella Sadhalá Avenue at the corner of República de Argentina Avenue, in Santiago de los Caballeros, Dominican Republic. Open in Google Maps.
What are your opening hours?
We are open every day, Monday to Sunday, from 12:00 noon to 12:00 midnight.
How do I make a reservation?
Message us on WhatsApp at +1 809-226-0026, or use the reservation form on this page, which writes the message for you with the date, time and party size.
What kind of food do you serve?
Fresh seafood (lobster, red snapper, shrimp, octopus, salmon), grilled Angus steaks (churrasco, ribeye, tomahawk, picanha), Dominican classics like mofongo, goat stew and oxtail, plus pastas, salads, burgers and desserts. See the full menu (in Spanish).
How much does it cost to eat at Amigos?
Starters from RD$ 495, main courses between RD$ 795 and RD$ 3,495, and desserts from RD$ 395. The 56 oz Angus Tomahawk, for sharing, is RD$ 6,995. Prices do not include taxes.
Is there parking?
Yes, we have ample parking for our guests.
Can I bring my pet?
Yes. We have a dedicated pet area, so you can come with your dog.
Do you deliver?
We don't offer delivery. Come visit us for the full Amigos experience.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all payment methods: cash, credit and debit cards, and bank transfers.
Do you have a play area for kids?
Yes. We have a kids' play area for children up to 12 years old, who must be supervised by an adult.
Can I host a birthday or private event?
Yes. We have private rooms and we set the table with your colors, flowers and decorations for birthdays, quinceañeras, graduations, anniversaries, weddings and corporate dinners. Request a quote.
Is it true Amigos holds a Guinness World Record?
Yes. In July 2025, in Santiago's Central Park, we prepared the world's largest mofongo, weighing 556 kg (1,226.8 lb), certified by Guinness World Records. We are the first Dominican restaurant to hold a Guinness World Record.
